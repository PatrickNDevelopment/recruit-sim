# Demo script

Run `npm run dev` and open http://localhost:5178/recruit-sim/.

## Journey 1: client discovery with a weak answer, review, stronger replay

1. **Scenarios → Client discovery call → Start a new attempt.**
2. Step 1: "Read the advert and the website…" (strong). Focus moves to "What happened".
3. Step 2: "Absolutely. I have great people. I’ll send five CVs this afternoon." (weak). The
   explanation says you promised CVs without knowing the role or asking any candidate.
4. Step 3: "Email Dana the five CVs from your database straight away." (harmful). Ending: **Trust lost
   before the search began**, with the candidate complaint as a consequence, kick-off readiness **Not
   ready** and candidate consent **Needs work**.
5. **Review explanations and mistakes**: two mistakes, each with the stronger option. **Replay from
   step 2** and play the strong answers to the end: **Call wrap-up**, readiness **Ready**, and "Try to
   start the search (practice)" passes. History shows both attempts.

## Journey 2: screening without consent cannot pass

1. **Candidate screening call → Start a new attempt.** Play: prepare, explain, probe the claims,
   be honest about pay, respect the salary-history refusal, then "I’ll send it over to the client this
   afternoon." (no consent), flag the clause, set next steps.
2. Ending: **Submission readiness: Not ready**, with only the consent item failing, although relevance
   and expectation-setting meet the standard. "Try to submit the candidate (practice)" says: _Blocked.
   Submission readiness is not met: Candidate consented to this specific submission._
3. **Replay from step 6 (Consent)**: ask, accept Mina’s "not yet", get the approved range, ask again and
   record her yes. Readiness becomes **Ready**.
